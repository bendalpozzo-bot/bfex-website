Step 2

Photos first.

Before any machine starts, take dated photos of every damaged thing: the fence line, the washed-out crossing, the silted dam, the trees down. Past NSW grant applications have asked for photographic evidence of direct damage, and photos taken after the clean-up cannot show it.

  • A wide shot and a close-up of each damaged item, with something in frame for scale.
  • Keep them together in one album on your phone, named for the event. The phone keeps the date on each photo.
  • Report the damage to the NSW Department of Primary Industries on its natural disaster damage survey: open the damage survey →
Step 3

Who helps with the paperwork.

BFEx does not fill in grant applications. These two services do, free of charge, for primary producers in NSW.

Rural Financial Counselling Service NSW, Northern Region
1800 344 090

Free and independent. Helps with accessing government assistance.

NSW Rural Assistance Authority
1800 678 593

Free call. The authority that runs NSW disaster assistance for producers; its staff help with lodging an application.

Not sure whether you count as a primary producer, or whether your insurance covers this? Ask either of them. The site visit below does not depend on the answer.
