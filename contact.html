What am I allowed to clear?

+

In designated bushfire-prone areas, the NSW 10/50 scheme lets you clear vegetation near your home without council approval. Within 10 metres of the house you can remove trees, stump included. Within 50 metres you can clear shrubs and undergrowth, but not trees.

Limits apply: slopes over 18 degrees, waterways, heritage items and some habitat are excluded, and you must confirm your property is in an entitlement area at rfs.nsw.gov.au on the day of clearing.

Before you start, the free RFS 10/50 eligibility checker and Boundary Clearing Tool are worth a look. If you are still not sure, call us. We work under 10/50, Local Land Services approvals and the Biodiversity Conservation Act daily and can usually tell you straight away.
