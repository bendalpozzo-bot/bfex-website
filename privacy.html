Privacy policy.

What we collect when you use this site, why we collect it, and how to get it deleted.

Last updated 22 September 2026

We're a small earthmoving business, not a data company. We collect the least we can get away with: enough to call you back about a job, and enough to know which pages of this site are worth keeping.

Who we are

This site is run by Bonville Forestry and Excavations Pty Ltd (ABN 83 654 611 364), trading as BFEx, of Coffs Harbour, NSW 2450, Australia. We are the ones responsible for the information described here.

  • Email — info@bfex.com.au
  • Phone — 0448 482 321

What we collect

When you ask for a quote

The quote form asks for your name and phone number, which services you're after, and an optional description of the job. That's all it asks for, and we don't ask for anything else on this site — no email field, no account, no payment details.

The form is delivered to us by Formspree, a third-party form service, which passes it to our inbox at info@bfex.com.au. Formspree keeps its own copy of each submission for a limited time on its servers in the United States; their handling is covered by their own privacy policy.

When you just browse

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people actually read. It records things like the page you viewed, roughly where in the world you are, and what kind of device you're on. It also records when someone taps a phone number, taps an email address, clicks one of our social icons, or submits the quote form.

Analytics never receives your name or phone number. We can see that a quote form was submitted; we can't match that to the form itself.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to tell repeat visits from new ones. We don't use advertising cookies, we don't run ad pixels on this site, and there's nothing here that follows you around the internet.

You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works fine either way.

Why we collect it

WhatWhat we do with it
Name, phone, job descriptionCall you back, quote the job, and do the work if you go ahead.
AnalyticsWork out which pages and services people care about, so we know what to write more of.

That's the whole list. We don't sell personal information, we don't rent it, and we don't hand it to marketers.

Who else sees it

  • Formspree — delivers the quote form to our inbox.
  • Google — provides the analytics described above.
  • OpenStreetMap — serves the small map on two of our service pages, so it sees your browser address the same way any image loaded from another site does. No tracking cookies.
  • Our email and accounting providers — where a quote becomes a real job, your contact details end up in our email and, if we invoice you, our accounting system.
  • Anyone we're legally required to tell — for example if a court or regulator compels it.

Formspree's servers are in the United States and Google's are worldwide, so your details do leave Australia. If you'd rather they didn't, ring or email us instead of using the form.

How long we keep it

Quote enquiries that don't turn into work stay in our inbox until you ask us to delete them (see below). Records for jobs we actually did are kept for seven years, because business records law requires it. Analytics data is kept on Google's standard retention setting.

Getting your information deleted

To have your personal information deleted, email info@bfex.com.au with the subject line "Delete my data".

Tell us the name and phone number you gave us so we can find the right record. We'll confirm and delete within 30 days, and we'll tell you when it's done.

If it's about our Facebook or Instagram posting tool, just say so — there's nothing to look up, and the same 30 days applies.

If we've done paid work for you, we have to keep the invoice and job records for seven years by law. We'll delete everything else and say exactly what we kept and why.

Seeing or correcting your information

Email the same address to ask what we hold about you, or to have something corrected. There's no charge, and we'll come back to you within 30 days.

Facebook and Instagram

We post on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. If you comment on or message those pages, the platform handles that under its own privacy policy, not this one; we see what any page owner sees.

We also run our own small posting tool, BFEx posting, connected to Meta. It publishes our reels to our Facebook Page and Instagram account, and reads the totals Meta gives any page owner — views, reach, likes, comments, follower counts. It does not read anything about you as an individual, and only we use it. To have anything it holds deleted, see Getting your information deleted.

Keeping it safe

Enquiries reach us over an encrypted connection and sit in password-protected accounts. We're a small business, not a bank — we take sensible precautions, but no system is perfectly secure and we won't pretend otherwise.

Children

This site is for people hiring earthmoving services. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

If something's wrong

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, email info@bfex.com.au — we'd rather fix it than argue about it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of the page. The current version is always the one published here.
