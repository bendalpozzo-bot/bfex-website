Common questions

How much does excavation cost?

It is priced per job after a look at the block. Access, the ground, what is being dug and how far the spoil has to go all change the time on site. Send a few photos and the address and we'll give you an honest quote, no obligation.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. A single trench run, a small shed pad or a one-day tidy-up gets quoted the same way as the big ones.

Can you take the spoil away?

Yes. The Hino tipper carts it to the tip, or we can stockpile or spread it on site if you would rather keep it.

Do I need approval to excavate?

It depends on what and where. A pad for a new shed or house usually sits under the approval your builder or designer is handling, and dams and some earthworks have their own rules. Ask us and we'll tell you if something looks like it needs a check before we start.
