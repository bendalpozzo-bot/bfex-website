The groundwork around the building

Civil earthworks is the work that makes a site usable and keeps water where it should be: forming access tracks and hardstands, laying culverts and building headwalls, cutting swales and drains, shaping and stabilising banks, and preparing ground for builders and fencers.

We do it with the Mecalac and the Hino, and we work alongside your builder, surveyor or engineer when the job has drawings.

What we do

  • Rural access tracks and hardstands
  • Culverts, headwalls and crossings
  • Stormwater lines and drainage trenches
  • Swales and surface drainage across tracks and paddocks
  • Erosion control and bank stabilisation
  • Site preparation and rehabilitation

Common questions

Do you work from engineering drawings?

Yes. Send the plans with your enquiry and we'll quote to them. For DA-related earthworks we work alongside your surveyor or builder.

Can you fix a track that washes out every wet season?

Usually the fix is drainage rather than more gravel: swales, a culvert, crowning the track so water runs off it. We look at where the water goes first, then quote the work.

Do you supply the gravel?

Yes. We cart gravel and road base in on the tipper, and culvert pipe and other materials are priced into the quote.
